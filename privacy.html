Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Anvil Isles ("the game"), made by Francois Sempe, collects and how it is used. If you have a question, write to francois@nomi.so.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your game progress (heroes, gear, ores, village, settings) is saved on your device only. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • Reminders ("your forge is ready") are scheduled on your device. They only appear if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • The game has no accounts, no chat and no way for players to talk to each other.

Purchases

Purchases (gems, the Starter Pack, No Ads, the Gem Card) are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, card or Apple Account details. The game only receives a receipt that says which item was bought, so it can give it to you and restore it later.

Ads

The game can show optional video ads, which you choose to watch for a reward. They are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Before any ad, the game asks for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). You can change your choice at any time with Settings > Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the game asks Apple's permission before Google may use your device's advertising identifier. If you say no, ads still work but are not personalized.
  • Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only with your permission), your IP address, device type and ad interactions, to show and measure ads. See how Google uses this information.
  • With No Ads, ad videos are never shown.

Analytics

The game sends usage events to PostHog (EU servers), such as a floor cleared or a purchase made, to help us improve the game. They carry a random id kept on your device, never your name, email or advertising identifier. PostHog may work out your approximate country or city from your IP address; it does not keep the IP address itself.

Purchases made through the App Store are also sent to RevenueCat, which counts sales and refunds for us. It receives the purchase (product, price, date) with its own random id, linked to the PostHog id, and never your name, email or payment details, which stay with Apple.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings > Send feedback, the face you pick and the words you type go to PostHog with the same random id. Please do not write your name, email or other personal details there.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can turn off personalized ads with Settings > Privacy choices or by refusing tracking in iOS Settings. To ask us to delete the analytics and feedback linked to your random id, write to francois@nomi.so. Deleting the game removes everything kept on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
