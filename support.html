Anvil Isles Support

Stuck on a floor, missing a purchase, or found a bug? Write to us and we will help.

Contact

Email francois@nomi.so. Telling us your device model and iOS version helps us answer faster. You can also send a quick note from Settings > Send feedback in the game.

Common questions

I bought something but did not get it.

Open the Shop or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. If it is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. The game has no account, so deleting the game deletes your village, heroes and gear. Items you bought (No Ads, the Starter Pack) come back with Restore Purchases.

How do I stop seeing ads?

Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. No Ads in the Shop gives you the rewards right away, without the video.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Open Settings > Privacy choices in the game.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Tap the Alerts tile in the game's Settings, or use iOS Settings > Notifications > Anvil Isles.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and pick the purchase.
